# Interview: Compliance Officer (Priya)

Recorded during discovery. Lightly edited.

"For any regulated or financial release the rule is written sign-off, in the
compliance log, before it goes out. A chat message saying 'looks fine' is not
sign-off and I've said so repeatedly. When I check the log against what actually
went out, there are gaps, releases distributed with no corresponding log entry.

The verbal-approval exception exists for genuinely time-critical items, maybe
once a month. My read is that it's being used far more often than that, as a
default rather than an exception, and the 24-hour retrospective logging rarely
happens.

I don't have visibility into the tracker's Approved column. I find out a release
went out when someone forwards me the published version. By then it's too late
to stop anything."
