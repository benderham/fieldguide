# Interview: Duty Editor (Sam)

Recorded during discovery. Lightly edited.

"Honestly the written sign-off step is more of a formality. For anything
financial I'll ping the compliance officer on chat, they say 'fine', and I move
it to Approved. We almost never wait for the formal log entry, there isn't time.
The tracker is the source of truth for me: if it's in the Approved column, it
goes out.

Most days I approve twenty to thirty releases myself. I don't loop in a second
senior editor unless something looks risky. The procedure says one senior
editor and that's usually me, so that's covered.

Re-keying is the annoying part. The client sends a Word doc, I copy the headline
and body into the tracker, then it gets copied again into the distribution tool.
Things get dropped or changed in that copying more than anyone admits."
