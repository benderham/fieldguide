# Press Release Approval Procedure (v3.1)

Owner: Editorial Operations. Last reviewed: 14 months ago.

1. Client submits a press release through the intake form or by email to the
   duty editor.
2. The duty editor performs an initial review for completeness and house style.
3. Any release naming a public company, a financial result, or a regulated
   product MUST receive written compliance sign-off before distribution. The
   sign-off is recorded in the compliance log.
4. Standard releases require approval from one senior editor. The approval is
   recorded by moving the item to the "Approved" column in the tracker.
5. Once approved, the release is scheduled for distribution across the wire,
   email, and partner channels.

Exceptions: time-critical releases may be distributed before written compliance
sign-off if a senior editor and the duty compliance officer both give verbal
approval. The verbal approval must be logged retrospectively within 24 hours.
