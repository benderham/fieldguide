# Brand Guidelines — Typography and Logo

**Primary typeface.** Söhne, in Buch and Kräftig weights. Fall back to Inter where Söhne is unavailable. Do not substitute Helvetica.

**Minimum sizes.** Body copy never below 16px on screen or 9pt in print. Captions may drop to 14px on screen.

**Logo clear space.** Maintain clear space equal to the height of the wordmark's lowercase "l" on all four sides. Do not place the logo on photography without the scrim treatment.

**Colour.** The full palette is in the Figma library. Do not sample colours from exported artwork.

**Don'ts.** Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, or add effects to the wordmark. Do not recreate it in another typeface. Do not use the 2019 mark under any circumstances.

For anything not covered here, ask the design lead before shipping.
