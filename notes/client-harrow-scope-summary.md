# Harrow — Scope Summary

Prepared for Harrow Financial Services. This document accompanies our proposal of 14 January.

**What we will deliver.** A redesigned customer onboarding journey across web and mobile web, comprising discovery, design, and build to production readiness. Six sprints of two weeks.

**Indicative investment.** £48,000 excluding VAT, subject to confirmation following discovery.

**What is not included.** Native applications. Ongoing support beyond the thirty-day defect window. Content production. Translation.

**Assumptions.** Harrow provides a single empowered decision-maker for design sign-off. Existing brand assets are supplied in editable form. Third-party identity verification is available in a test environment by the start of Sprint 2.

**Next steps.** On countersigning we will schedule a kickoff and confirm the discovery dates.

_This summary is indicative and does not vary the terms of the engagement letter._
