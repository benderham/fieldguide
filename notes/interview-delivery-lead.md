An interview with the delivery lead about what happens between a signed contract and the first sprint.

---

**Q: Walk me through what happens once a contract is signed.**

Honestly it depends who's running it. Normally there's a kickoff call in the first week or so — sometimes sooner if the client's keen, sometimes a lot later if we're mid-crunch on something else. Then we do a discovery block before anyone writes code.

**Q: How long is the discovery block?**

It's meant to be a couple of weeks. In practice it's whatever we can get away with. I've done four days. I've done three weeks once, but that was a bank.

**Q: And the estimate?**

We revise it after discovery, because the one in the proposal is always wrong. That's the whole point of doing discovery.

**Q: Who approves the revised estimate?**

Priya, usually? Or whoever owns the account. If it's a big jump we'd loop in Marcus, but I couldn't tell you what counts as big. Nobody's ever written that down as far as I know. It just sort of gets handled.

**Q: Anything that trips people up?**

Setting the project up in Harvest. Everyone forgets until finance chases.
