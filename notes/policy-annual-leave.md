# Annual Leave

_Owner: Operations. Last reviewed: January 2026._

- Twenty-five days per calendar year, plus public holidays. Five days may be carried over.
- Requests are made in BambooHR at least two weeks in advance. Longer than ten consecutive days needs your line manager's agreement first.
- We don't operate a shutdown period. Cover is arranged team by team.
- **Where a person holds an approval responsibility, they must record a delegate before leave begins.** Delegates are listed in the team calendar against the leave entry. Approvals must not be deferred until the approver returns, and must not be self-approved in their absence.
- Sickness is recorded in BambooHR on the first day of absence.
- Unused leave is not paid out except on termination.
