# Project Kickoff Procedure

_Owner: Operations. Last reviewed: March 2025._

All new client engagements follow the steps below. Deviations require written sign-off from the Managing Director.

1. **Contract countersigned.** Operations files the executed contract and notifies the delivery lead.
2. **Project created in Harvest.** Must be completed within two business days of countersigning, before any time is logged.
3. **Kickoff call scheduled.** To take place within five business days of countersigning.
4. **Discovery phase.** Two weeks, fixed. Produces a revised scope and a revised estimate.
5. **Estimate approval.** The revised estimate is approved by the **Managing Director** prior to the commencement of Sprint 1.
6. **Sprint 1 begins.**

Where a client requires an accelerated start, steps 3 and 4 may be compressed, but step 5 is not optional under any circumstances.
